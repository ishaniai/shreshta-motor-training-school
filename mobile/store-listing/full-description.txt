Shreshta Motor Training School — official app for new students to register and sign in for driving training.

FEATURES
• Easy online registration with secure account creation
• Login with username or email
• Choose training interest: 2 Wheeler, 4 Wheeler, or Both
• Driving license details capture when applicable
• Dark, easy-to-use interface designed for mobile

WHO IS THIS FOR?
Anyone aged 18+ who wants to enroll for motor training at Shreshta Motor Training School.

REGISTRATION INCLUDES
• Personal and contact details
• Address (all Indian states and union territories)
• Date of birth verification (18+ required)
• Optional driving license information
• Training preference selection

SECURITY
• Passwords are securely hashed
• Data transmitted over HTTPS
• Privacy policy available in-app listing

After registration, school administrators are notified. Log in anytime to access your training dashboard. More features coming soon!

Contact Shreshta Motor Training School for support and in-person training schedules.
